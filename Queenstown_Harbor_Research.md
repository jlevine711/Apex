## 2. The property today

Queenstown Harbor markets itself as a waterfront golf **resort** roughly an hour
from Washington, D.C. and Baltimore, and minutes over the Bay Bridge from
Annapolis. It pairs two championship courses with lodging and an events
business.([VisitMaryland](https://www.visitmaryland.org/listing/sports/queenstown-harbor-golf-links),
[qhgolf.com](https://qhgolf.com/golf/))

**The River Course** — the original layout — runs along the mouth of the Chester
River, with water in play on **14 of 18 holes** and panoramic Bay/river views on
more than half the course. It plays to par 72 at roughly **7,096 yards** (course
rating ~74.2), making it the sterner of the two
tests.([GolfDigest](https://www.golfdigest.com/courses/md/queenstown-harbor-the-river),
[foreTee](https://foretee.com/courses/maryland/queenstown/usa/atlantic-golf-at-queenstown-harbor--river/6614),
[Where2Golf](https://www.where2golf.com/usa-mid-atlantic/queenstown-harbor-river-course/))

**The Lakes Course** is the more forgiving, broadly playable layout (par 71,
~6,569 yards). It has repeatedly been named one of *Golf Digest*'s **Top 50
Public Courses for Women**. In **2024** it underwent a major bunker renovation —
42 traditional bunkers were removed and replaced with 20 grass-faced,
flat-bottom bunkers.([qhgolf.com](https://qhgolf.com/golf/),
[GolfDigest](https://www.golfdigest.com/courses/md/queenstown-harbor-the-lakes))

**Beyond golf:** a public driving range (Toptracer), two putting greens, a
chipping green and a practice bunker; the **River House & Tavern** event venue
with indoor space plus an outdoor patio/lawn overlooking the Chester River; and
on-site **cottages and bungalows** for overnight
stays.([qhgolf.com](https://qhgolf.com/golf/),
[Visit Queen Anne's](https://visitqueenannes.com/business/queenstown-harbor-golf/))

## 3. How it came about

### The land: from a colonial "thumb grant" to a dairy farm

The site carries a deep colonial history that the club itself foregrounds.
According to Queenstown Harbor's own account, the land was first granted in the
**1600s** to **Henry deCoursey**, a surveyor, by **Cecil Calvert, the second
Lord Baltimore**. As the story goes, deCoursey was asked to press his thumb onto
a map and was given all the land beneath the thumbprint — the tract became known
locally for centuries as the **"thumb grant"** (also recorded under the name
**"My Lord's Gift"**).([qhgolf.com — Who We Are](https://qhgolf.com/who-we-are/),
[MyEasternShoreMD](https://www.myeasternshoremd.com/news/queen_annes_county/book-tells-interesting-history-of-names/article_a269685c-2a1c-565b-b1a9-17716da29511.html))

> *Treat the thumb-grant story as the property's own historical narrative /
> local lore. The broader fact — that all early Maryland land flowed from
> grants issued by the Calvert proprietors — is well documented; the colorful
> thumbprint detail is the club's telling.*

By the time **The Brick Companies** found the property in the **1970s**, it was
in use as a **dairy farm and working crop fields**. Company executives decided
to build a golf course there specifically to open the land's beauty and wildlife
to the public.([qhgolf.com — Who We Are](https://qhgolf.com/who-we-are/),
[Where2Golf](https://www.where2golf.com/usa-mid-atlantic/queenstown-harbor-river-course/))

During construction, a large **archaeological dig** under the original deCoursey
home site recovered and preserved **millions of artifacts**. Many are displayed
at The Brick Companies' corporate headquarters in Edgewater, MD; many more were
donated to the **Maryland Historical
Trust**.([qhgolf.com — Who We Are](https://qhgolf.com/who-we-are/))

### Design and buildout timeline

The courses were designed by **Lindsay B. Ervin** of LBE Golf Course Design,
a Maryland-based architect.([LBE Golf Course
Design](https://www.lbegolf.com/queenstown),
[MSGA](https://msga.org/queenstown-harbor/))

- **1991** — The **River Course** opens as the original 18.
- **mid-1990s** — The facility expands to **27 holes** (a third nine is added).
- **1996** — A fourth nine completes the build; the third and fourth nines are
  combined into the second 18-hole layout, **The Lakes Course**, bringing the
  resort to its present **36 holes**.([Where2Golf](https://www.where2golf.com/usa-mid-atlantic/queenstown-harbor-river-course/),
  [GolfTheMidAtlantic](http://www.golfthemidatlantic.com/story/161))

> **Date discrepancy flagged:** golf-course databases and the proposal's own
> verified-facts list put the River Course opening at **1991**. The current
> owner's investor materials instead describe Queenstown Harbor as "operating
> since **1994**." The 1991 date is the more consistently cited across
> independent golf sources; 1994 may reflect when the multi-course destination
> was fully established.([Accountable Equity — Capital
> H6](https://accountableequity.com/capital-h6-hospitality-fund/))

## 4. The developer: The Brick Companies

Queenstown Harbor was conceived, built, and owned for ~30 years by **The Brick
Companies (TBC)** — a privately held, family-owned firm headquartered in
**Edgewater, Maryland**.([The Brick Companies — Golf
Courses](https://www.thebrickcompanies.com/golf-courses))

- **Founded 1892** as the **Washington Brick and Terra Cotta Company**, along
  the Washington City Canal in Southwest D.C. For decades its sole business was
  manufacturing brick and terra-cotta pipe.
- A **1939 fire** destroyed the plant; the company **never made another brick**,
  and instead diversified.
- It relocated its headquarters from D.C. to **Edgewater, MD in 2002**.
- Today TBC owns, develops and manages commercial, residential and recreational
  property across D.C., Maryland and Virginia — including golf courses
  (Queenstown Harbor, The Golf Club at South River) and **Atlantic Marinas**
  (Patapsco, Magothy, Ferry Point).([The Brick Companies —
  Legacy](https://www.thebrickcompanies.com/legacy))

This lineage matters for the funding question: the golf course was a
**diversification play by a cash-generating private real-estate company**, not a
venture-financed or publicly funded development.

## 5. How it was funded

### (a) The original development (1970s–1996)

The honest answer is that **no public construction-cost figure exists** —
The Brick Companies is privately held and has never disclosed what it spent to
build Queenstown Harbor. What the record *does* support:

- The land was **acquired by The Brick Companies** in the 1970s (former dairy
  farm) and developed in-house.([qhgolf.com — Who We
  Are](https://qhgolf.com/who-we-are/))
- TBC describes earlier business **partnerships** as having "provided the
  foundation and **financial platform** that has allowed the company to invest
  and diversify into **golf courses**, marinas and … real estate." In other
  words, the course was funded out of the company's own diversified
  balance sheet — **private, self-funded equity**, not outside project
  finance.([The Brick Companies — Legacy](https://www.thebrickcompanies.com/legacy))

**Bottom line:** Queenstown Harbor was a privately financed amenity/real-estate
development by an established family company. (If a hard development cost is
needed, it would have to come from TBC directly or from Queen Anne's County
land/permit records — not from public reporting.)

### (b) The May 2026 acquisition (~$25M)

In **May 2026**, **VIVÂMEE Hospitality, LLC** and its affiliate **Accountable
Equity, LLC** completed a combined **~$25 million** acquisition of **two**
Mid-Atlantic assets from The Brick Companies:

1. **Queenstown Harbor Golf Resort** (Queenstown, Eastern Shore), and
2. **The Golf Club at South River** (Edgewater, near Annapolis).

Together the portfolio comprises **54 holes** (Queenstown's 36 + South River's
18) across **870+ acres** of waterfront and conservation-protected
land.([Connect CRE](https://www.connectcre.com/stories/vivamee-hospitality-completes-25m-acquisition-of-two-maryland-golf-courses/),

**The financing / ownership structure:**

- **Seller:** The Brick Companies (confirmed via John Anderes, TBC's EVP of
  Hospitality & Golf, who appears in the transaction
  coverage).([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/847876/vivamee-hospitality-bolsters-maryland-golf-waterfront-portfolio-with-25-million-acquisition/))
- **Acquisition vehicle:** the deal was run **through Capital H6, LLC**, a
  single-purpose fund sponsored by **Accountable Equity,

  [Josh McCallen / LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshmccallen/))
- **Portfolio effect:** the closing expanded the VIVÂMEE platform to **six
  resort properties and five championship golf courses across Maryland and New

**Sponsor's investment thesis — [sponsor projection]:** Accountable Equity's

**lodging, events, weddings, corporate retreats and wellness** across the two
properties. *These are the sponsor's own projections from marketing collateral

diligenced.*([Accountable Equity — Capital H6](https://accountableequity.com/capital-h6-hospitality-fund/),

## 6. The sister asset: The Golf Club at South River

Acquired in the same $25M transaction (useful context for the H6 portfolio):

- **Location:** Edgewater, MD (Annapolis / South River side).
- **Course:** private 18-hole layout, designed by **Brian Ault**; opened as a
  public course in **1996** and **converted to private in 2007**.
- **Membership:** **600+ member families**, contributing recurring dues-based
  revenue.
- **Footprint:** ~165 acres, with 8 lakes and multiple protected
  areas.([GolfPass / GolfLink](https://www.golflink.com/golf-courses/md/edgewater/golf-club-at-south-river),
  [Club + Resort Business](https://clubandresortbusiness.com/vivamee-hospitality-acquires-two-maryland-golf-locations-for-25m/),
  [golfclubsr.com](https://www.golfclubsr.com/golf))
